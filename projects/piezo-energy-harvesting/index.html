Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting

Finite-element models of PZT harvesters built into a wheel, and a digital-twin plan to estimate harvestable power from a robot's suspension.

Sep – Dec 2025 · Smart Materials Lab, University of Maryland

  • PZT 1.0, 2.0, wheelModels
  • ANSYS + COMSOLSolvers
  • Isaac Sim twinNext

Problem

Estimate how much energy piezoelectric stacks could recover from wheel and suspension loads before building hardware.

Approach

  1. Built coupled piezoelectric models in ANSYS (two design iterations), then a PZT wheel model in ANSYS and COMSOL.
  2. Planned a digital twin in NVIDIA Isaac Sim: a Husky UGV over KITTI terrain at 400 Hz, logging suspension joint forces.
  3. Defined the post-processing: open-circuit voltage from the d33 constitutive model for PZT-5A, and power into a matched load.

Results

  • Validated model setups in ANSYS and COMSOL; the Isaac Sim phase is the next step.

Tools

  • ANSYS Mechanical / APDL
  • COMSOL
  • NVIDIA Isaac Sim
  • Python
