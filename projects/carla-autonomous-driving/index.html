CARLA Autonomous Navigation

An autonomous-driving stack in CARLA that fuses LiDAR and camera data to detect obstacles, then decides and plans maneuvers like following and overtaking.

Sep – Dec 2025 · Team of 5 · ENPM818Z

● LIVE SIM
  • LiDAR + cameraSensors
  • CARLA 0.9.16Simulator
  • 5Team

Problem

A self-driving car has to see dynamic obstacles reliably, decide what to do, and produce a smooth, feasible path, all in real time.

Approach

  1. Built data pipelines that fuse LiDAR and camera data to detect dynamic obstacles and model environmental uncertainty.
  2. Used Markov Decision Process methods and a behavior tree for high-level maneuver decisions; I implemented the behavior-tree helper, condition and action nodes.
  3. Planned trajectories in the Frenet frame: quintic lateral and quartic longitudinal polynomials, jerk-based costs and feasibility checks.
  4. Validated scenarios for an empty road, car following and overtaking.

Results

  • Working follow and overtake behavior across the tested scenarios, with trajectory plots for each.
