Tactile-Guided Insertion

A simulated Panda arm learns round-nut insertion from 172 teleoperated demos, using touch from simulated DIGIT sensors to handle the contact-rich final millimeters.

Spring 2026 · Joint project with Pranav Deshakulkarni Manjunath · ENPM690 Robot Learning

  • 172Demos
  • 64-d / fingerTactile signal
  • BC-RNN + GMMPolicy

Problem

Insertion fails at the end, where the camera view is blocked and contact decides success. We wanted a policy that feels its way in, trained from a small demo set.

Approach

  1. Simulated DIGIT tactile images with FOTS on a custom Panda gripper in robosuite (MuJoCo).
  2. Compressed each tactile image to a 64-d signal with a training-free pipeline: baseline subtraction, grayscale and 8×8 pooling.
  3. Trained a robomimic BC-RNN policy (2-layer LSTM, hidden size 1000) with a 5-mode GMM action head on a 151-d observation, on the Zaratan HPC cluster.
  4. Found that without gradient clipping and learning-rate decay, validation loss diverged to about 90,000 with gradient norms near 6×10⁸; with both, training converged.

Results

  • Validation action error (MAE) of 0.205 on 17 held-out demos.
  • Pick and approach succeeded in about 80% of rollouts; full insertion in 10% (5 of 50). Remaining failures are in final seating, the next target.
