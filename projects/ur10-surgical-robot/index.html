UR10 Surgical Assistant Robot

A UR10-based surgical assistant with a custom gripper and instrument set, taken from SolidWorks CAD to a URDF model running in Gazebo.

Nov 2024 – Jan 2025 · ENPM662 Introduction to Robot Modeling

  • 20+Model iterations
  • 4Instruments
  • CAD → URDF → GazeboPipeline

Problem

Hand a surgeon the right instrument: the arm, gripper and tools all need accurate geometry and kinematics before any control work.

Approach

  1. Modeled the arm, a custom gripper and four instruments (scalpel, scissors, syringe, tray) in SolidWorks.
  2. Exported to URDF with sw2urdf and iterated the model through more than 20 versions.
  3. Simulated in Gazebo with ROS launch files and worked out forward and inverse kinematics in Python and MATLAB.

Results

  • A working simulated model of the arm, gripper and instruments in Gazebo.
