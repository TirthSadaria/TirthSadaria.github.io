VR Human-in-the-Loop Assistive Feeding

I put a real person into an assistive-feeding simulation through a VR headset to test whether robot policies trained on still, simulated humans hold up when the person moves.

Fall 2025 · Team of 3 with Amogha Thalihalla Sunil and Shreya Kalyanaraman · ENPM645

  • Quest 2Headset
  • 6-DOFTracking
  • Assistive GymSimulator

Problem

Feeding-assist policies are trained against simulated people who sit still. Real users lean and turn their heads.

Approach

  1. Integrated an Oculus Quest 2 with SteamVR and PyBullet (Assistive Gym).
  2. Mapped 6-DOF head and controller tracking onto the simulated person in real time.
  3. Built fail-safes that freeze the simulation when tracking becomes erratic.
  4. As a team, evaluated pretrained feeding policies with a VR participant in the loop, with a data-capture and metrics pipeline.

Results

  • Policies trained on static humans failed once the user leaned or rotated, which shows the need for human motion during training and evaluation.

Tools

  • Oculus Quest 2
  • SteamVR
  • PyBullet
  • Assistive Gym
  • Python

Built on the open-source Assistive Gym and Assistive VR Gym projects.
